HomeHuddle Support

We're here to help. Most questions are answered below.

Contact us

Email hello@venuewise.net — we typically reply within one business day.

Getting started

  • Sign in with your mobile number — we text you a one-time code (no password).
  • Add your family and each child's schedule feed to see everything in one calendar.
  • Turn on notifications to get reminders before games and practices.

Subscriptions & billing

  • HomeHuddle is $9.99/month after a 14-day free trial. The first 100 Founding Families lock in $4.99/month while continuously subscribed.
  • On iPhone/iPad: manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use “Restore Purchases” on the Account screen if a purchase isn't showing.
  • On the web: manage billing from your Account page at venuewise.net.

Delete your account

Open Account → Delete account in the app or on the website. Deletion is initiated by the family owner and completes after a 7-day grace period, during which you can cancel it by signing back in.
