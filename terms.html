The Service

HomeHuddle syncs your family's sports schedules from third-party calendar apps (such as GameChanger and TeamSnap) and delivers schedule alerts via SMS and push notifications. The service is intended for use by parents and guardians managing their family's sports schedules.

HomeHuddle is provided on a subscription basis. After a free 14-day trial, continued use requires a paid subscription. SMS Messaging Terms

By providing your mobile phone number and signing up for HomeHuddle, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from HomeHuddle (powered by Venuewise), including:

  • Schedule change alerts when game times or locations are updated

To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, text START to the same number. For help, reply HELP or contact us at hello@venuewise.net.
